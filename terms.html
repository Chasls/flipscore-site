What FlipScore is

FlipScore is a browser extension that estimates what secondhand items sell for, using public sold-price information from eBay and other sites, and helps you decide whether a listing is worth buying to resell. It is an independent tool, not affiliated with eBay, Facebook, Craigslist, OfferUp, PriceCharting, or Swappa.

Estimates, not advice

Prices, grades, profits, fees, and offers shown by FlipScore are estimates based on recent public sales and the settings you choose. They can be wrong: listings can be mismatched, sites change, fees change, and markets move. Check items and prices yourself before you buy or sell. FlipScore isn't financial, tax, or legal advice; the flip tracker's CSV is a convenience, not a tax filing.

Using FlipScore responsibly

FlipScore reads pages you visit and looks up prices from your own browser, at a deliberately slow pace. You're responsible for how you use it and for following the terms of the sites you use. Don't use FlipScore to harass sellers, to scrape sites at scale, or for anything unlawful.
